# Needs and growth

## In charge of

The friend's needs and how it develops: hunger, feeding, weight,
energy, mood, and growing up over time.

## Not in charge of

- How the body shows weight: `friend-body`.
- The act of feeding: `care-touch`.
- Skills and stats grown through play: `play`.
- Time passing while the app is closed: `time-save` supplies it.

## Seed notes (from Mikey's first ideas, unconfirmed)

- Overfeed and it gets really big; underfeed and it gets skinny.

## First questions

- Can a friend die, or only become sad, weak or worn?
- How much should needs nag the player? Tamagotchi-strict, or gentle?
- Do stuffed friends eat food, or something else?
