# 0033: Skills grow by repetition, quietly

## Decision

Friends get better at things by doing them: catching, kicking,
bouncing on a trampoline. Skills grow on the side of play; their
numbers stay mostly hidden, only implied by how the friend does. No one
is pushed to pick a toy for its stat.

## Context

Recording 2 (`inbox/2026-10-24-recording-2-part1.md` and `-part2.md`), confirmed point by point on 2026-10-25. Mikey: "playing can build skills on the side, for sure...
more casual players, they're not going to care so much about that...
We don't really even want to show those skill stats to the players too
much, but it can be kind of implied."

## Why

- Builds on 0017 (play, not train): experienced players can notice and
  use it; casual players just play.
- Even a skilled friend still fumbles now and then.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Visible stat bars per toy:** turns play into training.

## Consequences

- `play` and `needs-growth`: skills rise with repetition; mistakes stay
  possible. The sketch's ball is mastered too fast (0021).
