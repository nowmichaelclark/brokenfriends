# 0032: Almost no text

## Decision

The game plays without reading. Text is for settings menus and little
else. The sketch's hint text is temporary.

## Context

Playing the sketch, Mikey could tell how his friend was doing from its
body alone: "the text at the top can maybe even go away completely...
that would also kind of open it up to be easier to play for people who
don't speak English or people who can't read."

## Why

- Pillar 1: the body is the interface.
- Kids who can't read yet, and players of any language, can play.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Text hints and labels throughout:** a crutch, and a language wall.

## Consequences

- Tutorials, quest risk (0034), and what happened while away (0029)
  must all be shown, not written. `ui-ux` owns this.
