# 0026: The head is the friend; parts outlive it

## Decision

- **The personality lives in the head**, with the chip brain. A friend
  can keep living on a new body.
- **Taking a head from a friend** leads straight into sewing that head
  onto a body as well (often the body the other head left). Or the
  player can throw the head away, or keep it; a head without a body
  dies after about a day.
- **A dead friend's parts can be reused.** Died at camp: nearly all
  of its parts. Died on a quest: one or two (something scavenged the
  rest). The friend itself never comes back (0011).

## Context

Recording 2 (the 2026-10-24 recording (digested; see git history)), confirmed point by point on 2026-10-25. Q-017 asked about keepsakes and rebuilding from a dead
friend. Mikey: "you take another creature and you rip its head off.
And the creature whose personality you like, you put its head on the
other creature's body."

## Why

- Saving a friend whose body is failing is a strong, strange, very
  Frankenstein moment (a visual touchstone).
- Parts outliving their friend makes death matter without wasting it.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Only a few keepsakes survive any death (0011's rule):** narrowed
  here for deaths at camp.

## Consequences

- Narrows 0011's keepsake rule; 0011 otherwise stands.
- Resolves Q-017. Open: why a loose head dies (perhaps a torn-off head
  rots faster), in `open-questions.md`.
