# 0002: The game is 2D

## Decision

The game is entirely 2D.

## Context

The engine, physics and art choices all depend on this.

## Why

Mikey: "the game is completely 2D." Tearing, sewing and weight changes
are far simpler for a solo developer in 2D, and side-on carrying over
terrain suits it.

## Alternatives rejected

- **3D:** much more work for body deformation, art and animation.

## Consequences

Easier: body mechanics, art, performance on cheap phones and in
browsers. Harder: nothing significant known yet.
