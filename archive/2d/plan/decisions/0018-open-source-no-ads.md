# 0018: Open source, free, no ads

## Decision

Broken Friends is open source and free to play, with no ads.

## Context

Decision 0014 made the game free, which raised Q-024: are there ads?

## Why

Mikey: "No, it will not have ads. This is going to be an open source
and free to play game. As much as it can be anyways."

## Alternatives rejected

- **Ads:** they pull the player away and hold attention, which pillar 4
  refuses.
- **Closed source:** not what Mikey wants for a hobby project.

## Consequences

- No ads, shop or tracking. "As much as it can be": app stores may
  still charge Mikey fees; that doesn't change the game.
- A licence is needed for code, art and music, and it must fit
  Driftloom's core (Q-025).
