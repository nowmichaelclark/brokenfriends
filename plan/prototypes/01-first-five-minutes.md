# Prototype 1: the first five minutes

The first real build of the game (decision 0019), in `game/`. Not
throwaway: it's the start of the game itself.

## The question

Is it satisfying to rip off a rotting part, sew on a new one, clean and
feed a friend, and then play with it? Does the body alone show how
the friend is doing (pillar 1)?

## Smallest build

The first five minutes from `inbox/2026-10-24-recording-1.md`: one
friend in the tent, tearing the rotting leg, the bin, the spare in a
pocket, a four-layer surgery, the dangling eye, sponge, towel, stuffing,
oil, poke, tickle, pet, and the play corner with a ball they learn to
catch. Full list in `game/README.md`.

## Out of scope

Quests, other camps, time away, more than one friend, Driftloom
music, donation, keepsakes, death.

## How Mikey will judge it

Play it on the phone for ten minutes, then answer:
- Is tearing the leg off fun? Too easy, too hard, too gross, not gross
  enough?
- Does surgery feel like Trauma Center, or like a chore?
- Can you tell how your friend is doing without the hints?
- Does poking and petting make you like them?
- Is the ball fun for more than a minute?
- What's the first thing you'd change?

## Result

Mikey played it on his phone, in the browser and as the Android app
(recording 2, 2026-10-24). It runs at 60 fps.

- **Tearing:** very fun, the fluff falling out especially; not too
  gross. Odd that healthy limbs wouldn't come off (now 0025). The fluid
  leak where the leg was barely showed.
- **Surgery:** felt like Trauma Center. A bit easy (fine for the first)
  and the camera never got close enough to see the stitches (0031).
- **Without the hints:** yes, the body showed it all (shaking, mouth,
  sad eyes). The text can go (0032).
- **Poking and petting:** cute, fun, funny.
- **The ball:** fun for about 20 seconds. The friend learned to catch
  too fast and never fumbled after; the ball only hit the head (it
  should hit any part, and a friend could kick it back) (0033).
- **Sounds:** the voices were a highlight. Music changing every loop
  was wrong (0038).
- **First change:** the look. Limbs read as a paper doll, not a fluffy
  friend (0037).

Verdict: "a really fun sketch to start with", and still a sketch
(0021).
