# 0010: No gating, no microtransactions, both play patterns

## Decision

Broken Friends supports two play patterns, neither second class: short
daily or weekly check-ins, and longer sessions with more creatures and
more to do. Nothing is gated, and there are no microtransactions.

## Context

The first Brain session asked who the game is for and how they play.
Mikey: "Nothing is gated, no microtransactions. We're not trying to
hold the player to something."

## Why

- Attachment is a better reason to return than pressure, and it fits
  the pitch's feeling.
- Free of pressure, the odd, wholesome tone stays intact.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Energy or timer gates:** they hold the player to something.
- **Daily streaks with rewards:** another way of holding the player.

## Consequences

- Shapes `00-vision/pitch.md` (audience and play patterns) and should
  inform `00-vision/non-goals.md`.
- Time away can still change a creature, and a player can always get a
  new creature. How that stays a change rather than a loss, and whether
  a weekly player can lose a creature they love, is open (Q-011).
- Narrows Q-005: no microtransactions, but free or paid is still open.
