# 0037: Friends are made by maths

## Decision

- **Friends are generated by maths**, weighted like Driftloom: common
  and rare features, with sliders for the developers.
- **Great variety that still looks like one game**: many kinds of
  head, and friends that aren't animals (monsters, demons, aliens,
  ghost-like designs that are still plush, never actual ghosts).
- **Bodies move by their build**: thin friends wobble, plump ones
  bounce, thin ears droop and swing; hair has physics (like Sally's).
- **Every friend sounds different**, across thousands of friends.

## Context

Q-006 asked how much art is generated. Mikey: "I really like art that
is generated by code... how can we kind of do that as a math equation
or as a math problem?" He found the sketch's limbs "look kind of like a
2D paper doll rather than like a kind of fluffy creature", and wants
the look to stay cheap enough for weak phones.

## Why

- Generated friends make a collection of thousands possible, and parts
  that move between friends need one shared way of drawing.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Hand-drawn friends:** too few, and swapped parts wouldn't match.

## Consequences

- Resolves Q-006. `art-direction` and `friend-body` design the
  generator; the sketch's look will be redone (0021).
