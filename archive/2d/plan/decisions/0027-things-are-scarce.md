# 0027: Things are scarce

## Decision

Parts, toys and tools are found on quests, not lying around. Supplies
run out too, even stuffing and oil. The player chooses which friends to
care for most, and a neglected friend may die.

## Context

Recording 2 (the 2026-10-24 recording (digested; see git history)), confirmed point by point on 2026-10-25. Mikey: "stuffing shouldn't just be something the player
has an infinite amount of, or like the oil... So the player is going
to have to pick and choose maybe like which friends to really take
care of."

## Why

- Scarcity gives quests their point and makes care a choice.
- The first five minutes still hands over a spare leg; later, parts
  are rare finds.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Unlimited stuffing and oil:** care becomes free and choices vanish.

## Consequences

- How scarce is a tuning question (`open-questions.md`).
- Watch pillar 4 (nothing holds the player): scarcity must never
  become a wall.
