# 0014: Broken Friends is free

## Decision

The game is free to play, with no microtransactions (decision 0010).

## Context

Q-005 asked whether the game is free or a one-time purchase.

## Why

Mikey: "free". It's a hobby project, and nothing in it should hold or
charge the player.

## Alternatives rejected

- **A one-time purchase:** a barrier to trying it.

## Consequences

- No shop and no currency bought with money.
- Whether there are ads is not yet decided; the non-goals session
  should settle it.
