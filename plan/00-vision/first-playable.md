# First playable

The smallest version of the game that is still the game: the first
version someone else could play and enjoy. Not the first prototype
(that's just the toy).

Mikey (2026-10-25), after playing the sketch: "it was like that
already feels like the first version. The biggest thing that's missing
for sure is quests... even if we just have one camp, we need to be
able to have quests and friends that can come in and friends that can
die... I kind of think that core loop is already enough."

## In

Mikey's list:

- **The care of the first five minutes:** tearing, surgery, washing,
  feeding, poking, play (prototype 1), reworked as needed (0021).
- **One camp.**
- **Quests** shown on a map, with a route and a visible risk (0034).
- **New friends arriving** at camp (0034).
- **Friends dying** (0028), with their parts left to reuse (0026).

What those need to work (Claude's reading, for Mikey to confirm):

- Scarce supplies, so quests bring back something worth having (0027).
- Time passing while away, shown by looking (0029).
- Little or no text (0032).

## Out, on purpose (proposed, for Mikey to confirm)

- **More camps, biomes and the ending** (0035): the core loop must be
  fun at one camp first.
- **Sharing friends by code** (0039) and the **friend maker** for
  players (0036).
- **Chores** (0041): they matter once there are several camps.
- **A full generated cast** (0037). But body plans come in early and
  as many as can be built, since this version is a dev release with
  everything open (proposed 0045).
- **Drawing routes freehand** (0034): the first playable offers a few
  preset routes per quest, each with its own risk (Gate A).
