# Care and touch

## In charge of

Everything the player does to a friend with a finger: poking,
dragging, tickling, feeding, washing, drying, tearing and sewing, and
how each gesture feels.

## Not in charge of

- What the friend's body does in response: `friend-body`.
- What the action changes in the material: `condition`.
- Menus and buttons: `ui-ux`.

## Seed notes (from Mikey's first ideas, unconfirmed)

- Pull limbs apart; sew them back together.
- Claude's suggestion: sewing could be a small tracing gesture or
  minigame.

- Surgery has layers (recording 1, 2026-10-24): clean away fluff, cut
  the rip tidy, sew, maybe a flap system, reconnect the liquid-filled
  ligaments and soak up spills with a cloth; a dangling eye button
  gets its own short sewing game. Trauma Center on DS is the feel.
  Recording 2: the first surgery may be easy, but the camera must zoom
  in closer, to the stitches (does that need vector drawing?).
- A torn-off part is dragged and tossed into a trash can; a spare
  waits as an icon at the screen's side and enlarges when tapped
  (recording 1). The toss could grow into a mini game.
- Surgery happens lying down in its own place, apart from the tent
  where friends are washed and dressed (recording 2).
- Washing and drying (towel, hair dryer). Feeding is stuffing (a joke
  on holiday stuffing) and liquid or oil, needed now and then as a
  friend dries out (recording 1).
- Touch: a sore spot gets a head shake and a sound, a tickle spot a
  giggle, a liked spot a glow and a jiggle; petting makes a friend
  purr and close its eyes; the belly has a soft teddy bounce
  (recordings 1 and 2).

## First questions

- Which gestures exist, and what does each do?
- Is tearing something the player chooses, an accident, or both?
- What does the friend do when you're rough with it?
