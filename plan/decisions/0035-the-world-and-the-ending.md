# 0035: The world, its camps, and the ending

## Decision

- **The open world is a map only**, and it is in the game from early on.
- **Many camps**: at least one per biome, ideally an easy, a hard and
  an extreme one. Harder camps have fewer resources but bigger quest
  rewards.
- **New camps are found** by questing further out, and **built from
  materials** (several kinds or amounts per camp; no currency).
- **Biomes reward** new kinds of friend and biome-only toys.
- **No campaign. One ending**: when every camp is unlocked a last camp
  opens, and unlocking it plays the ending scene. Play goes on after,
  and that late game should be the game at its most fun.

## Context

Q-018 (scope of camps and the open world) and Q-019 (story). Recording 2 (`inbox/2026-10-24-recording-2-part1.md` and `-part2.md`), confirmed point by point on 2026-10-25.
Mikey also dropped one proposal: limits on moving friends between camps.

## Why

- Biomes and their friends give quests direction and variety.
- An ending gives the loose arc (be a good doctor, set up camps,
  befriend everyone) a moment without a plot.

## Alternatives rejected

- **A currency to unlock camps:** Mikey prefers materials.
- **Limits on moving friends between camps:** dropped.

## Consequences

- Resolves Q-018 and Q-019. Narrows the "no story campaign" non-goal:
  one ending scene is allowed. Open: which materials.
