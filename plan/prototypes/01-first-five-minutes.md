# Prototype 1: the first five minutes

The first real build of the game (decision 0019), in `game/`. Not
throwaway: it's the start of the game itself.

## The question

Is it satisfying to rip off a rotting part, sew on a new one, clean and
feed a creature, and then play with it? Does the body alone show how
the creature is doing (pillar 1)?

## Smallest build

The first five minutes from `inbox/2026-10-24-recording-1.md`: one
creature in the tent, tearing the rotting leg, the bin, the spare in a
pocket, a four-layer surgery, the dangling eye, sponge, towel, stuffing,
oil, poke, tickle, pet, and the play corner with a ball they learn to
catch. Full list in `game/README.md`.

## Out of scope

Quests, other camps, time away, more than one creature, Driftloom
music, donation, keepsakes, death.

## How Mikey will judge it

Play it on the phone for ten minutes, then answer:
- Is tearing the leg off fun? Too easy, too hard, too gross, not gross
  enough?
- Does surgery feel like Trauma Center, or like a chore?
- Can you tell how your friend is doing without the hints?
- Does poking and petting make you like them?
- Is the ball fun for more than a minute?
- What's the first thing you'd change?
