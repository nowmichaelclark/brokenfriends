# Condition

## In charge of

What state the friend's material is in: wetness, mold, dirt, tears,
and how much stuffing it has, and how each changes over time.

## Not in charge of

- The body's parts and seams themselves: `friend-body`.
- The player's actions that change condition (washing, drying,
  sewing): `care-touch`.
- Hunger and weight: `needs-growth`.

## Seed notes (from Mikey's first ideas, unconfirmed)

- Friends can get wet, grow mold, and lose fluff that falls out.
- Claude's suggestion: wet means heavier and slower; staying wet too
  long grows mold.

## First questions

- Is condition ever permanent, or can everything be repaired?
- How does condition differ between land and water species?
- What is the difference between stuffing and fluff, if any?
