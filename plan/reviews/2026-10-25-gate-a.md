# Review: Gate A, the vision (Claude Opus 5.5)

## Verdict

**Go, after these fixes** (all small; done in the same session):

1. Scarcity (0027) can break pillar 5: if cleaning or treating supplies
   run out, rot crosses a camp the player has no way to clean. Keep one
   basic way to clean and treat that never runs out; scarcity makes care
   slower and choosier, never impossible. Built as an assumption and
   asked (game/README).
2. Q-023 asked for a fun pillar and none is written. Draft pillar 6
   ("friends are fun to touch") for Mikey to confirm.
3. `pillars.md` "Not pillars" still calls donation, keepsakes, the open
   world and multiple camps parked; they are decided (0025, 0026, 0035).
4. Cut the first playable's riskiest scope: preset routes instead of
   freehand route drawing, and a small generator (a handful of body
   plans) instead of the full cast. Both stay in the game for later.

## Checks that passed

- **One coherent game?** Yes. Care for stitched friends at camp, send
  them on quests to find what care needs, accept that they change and
  die. Every decision from 0008 to 0041 points the same way.
- **Pillars are real tests?** Yes; each names a feature it rejects, and
  the new decisions pass them: head swaps and reused parts never undo a
  friend (pillar 2); every death has a visible cause (0028, pillar 3);
  0024 and 0029 are pillar 4 in practice; 0032 is pillar 1 taken all
  the way.
- **Non-goals hold back the real temptations?** Yes: monetisation,
  engagement hooks, weapons, online play, text, campaign. The one open
  edge (rotas and schedules) is Q-029.
- **Can one person build the first playable?** Yes, with fix 4. One
  camp, the existing care loop, quests on a simple map, arrivals and
  death. The sketch already proves the hardest technical parts: the
  look runs at 60 fps on a 2022 budget phone, and Driftloom's music
  plays in the browser and the Android app.
- No decision contradicts another. 0021 and 0026 narrow 0019 and 0011
  without superseding them; the narrowing is stated in each.

## Rubric grade

- pitch, non-goals, first-playable: drafted from Mikey's own words;
  first-playable's "Out" list waits on Mikey (Q-030).
- pillars: five tested pillars; the sixth drafted here.
- references: not confirmed (finding 6).

## Weak spots in this review

- The same model drafted much of what it reviewed, and no red team from
  another lab ran first (`kit/PHASES.md` asks for one). Mikey chose to
  move to building; a red team of the vision can run alongside.
- Feasibility of the full game (generated friends that swap parts,
  thousands of voices, quest simulation offline) is a Gate B and C
  question, not checked here.
