# 0028: How friends die, and what time away does

## Decision

- **A friend dies when its brain is lost**: by not surviving a quest, or
  by falling apart at camp until rot, missing stuffing, leaking fluid,
  bugs, worms, mold, water or rust reach it. Not easy, but a
  semi-regular part of normal play.
- **Time away (Q-021)**: how tidy the camp was left decides how likely
  loss is. Hunger never kills, but it speeds rot and makes quests more
  likely to fail. Mold left on a rotting part spreads to other friends.

## Context

Recording 2 (`inbox/2026-10-24-recording-2-part1.md` and `-part2.md`), confirmed point by point on 2026-10-25. Mikey: "It shouldn't necessarily be easy for it to die
because these are creatures that players are going to get attached to...
But at the same time, it should definitely be able to die and it should
be a, we could say, a semi-regular part of a normal player's
experience." The time-away rules are his answer recorded in
`inbox/2026-10-24-mikey-answers.md`, confirmed.

## Why

- Every death has a visible cause (pillar 3).
- Builds on 0011: absence alone never kills a healthy friend.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Death as a rare event:** Mikey wants it part of normal play.

## Consequences

- Resolves Q-021. `condition` and `time-save` take these rules.
