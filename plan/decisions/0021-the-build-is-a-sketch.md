# 0021: The current build is a sketch; the plans lead

## Decision

Everything in `game/` today is a sketch. Any of it, up to all of it, may
be reworked or thrown out as planning settles what the game is. The
plans decide the game; the build never decides the plans.

This narrows decision 0019's line "Phases 3 onward now refine a working
build instead of starting one": later phases may refine the build or
start parts again, whichever the plans call for. 0019 otherwise stands
(building continues alongside planning).

## Context

Mikey, 2026-10-24, after playing the build on his phone and installing
the Android app: "I would like for us to look at what we currently have
as just a sketch, so like any and all of what is currently live [may]
need to be completely reworked even depending upon how the planning
goes. Still, this is a really fun sketch to start with."

## Why

- The build was made from one recording and many guesses (the
  "Assumed in this build" list in `game/README.md`).
- Planning hasn't passed Gate A; pillars, non-goals and the first
  playable can still change what the game is.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Treat the build as the base to grow from:** code shaped by guesses
  would quietly steer the design.

## Consequences

- A planning session never treats "the build already does X" as a
  reason. The build is evidence about feel, not a decision.
- What carries over is what was learned: what felt good on the phone,
  and the performance practice (`systems/platforms-tech/`).
- On acceptance, 0019's status stays accepted; this narrows one of its
  consequences rather than replacing it.
