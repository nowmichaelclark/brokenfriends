# Art direction

## In charge of

The look: style, palette, materials, how friends are drawn and
animated, and how wetness, mold and tears show.

## Not in charge of

- The rules behind what is shown: `friend-body` and `condition`.

## Seed notes (from Mikey's first ideas, unconfirmed)

- Stuffed, stitched friends; references: the films *9* and *The
  Nightmare Before Christmas*.
- Claude's suggestions: 2D cut-out parts on a skeleton; a fabric and
  stitch look like LittleBigPlanet or Kirby's Epic Yarn.

- Friends are made by maths, weighted like Driftloom, with great
  variety that still looks like one game; bodies move by their build
  (decision 0037). Front and back views only (0031). Visual
  touchstones also include *Frankenstein*.

- The sketch's friends read as 2D paper dolls; wanted: many head shapes
  and sizes, silly, demon, ghost and alien looks, fur of different
  lengths on some parts or all over, and hair with its own physics
  like Sally's (recording 2, 2026-10-24; looks note, 2026-10-25). Parts
  such as ears, noses and eyes must also detach and swap.
- Claude's read of the first generated sheet (54 friends, 2026-10-25;
  `game/tests/gallery.gd --by_plan`), which Mikey agreed with: every
  plan shares one body outline and tube limbs, and every head is an
  oval; monsters read as purple cats; floppy ears stick out sideways;
  the ghost-like hem reads as feet; every eye is the same button;
  colours are random within a plan; no hair, clothes or lopsided
  patchwork yet. Mikey: no redesign yet (agenda S-008).

## First questions

- How is a friend generated so that parts still fit when swapped
  between friends (0025, 0037)?
- Cute, eerie, or both?
- How much of the look can come from shaders instead of drawings?
