# 0031: Front and back only; surgery on its own table

## Decision

- **Friends are seen from the front and the back, never the side.** In
  the play area they walk toward and away from the screen, and simply
  flip to their back to walk away.
- **The back is used too**: tail surgery, brain surgery, capes.
- **Surgery happens lying down, in its own place**, with its own tools.
  The standing view is for washing and accessories.

## Context

Recording 2 (`inbox/2026-10-24-recording-2-part1.md` and `-part2.md`), confirmed point by point on 2026-10-25. Mikey: "I think it's almost kind of charming to only have
like the front view mainly and the back view." On surgery: "they should
lay down... it'll make it easier for us to kind of have more tools for
the surgery."

## Why

- Two views keep generated art and part swapping manageable.
- A separate surgery place can zoom close (Mikey: the sketch's camera
  was too far away) and hold more tools.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Side views:** a lot of engineering for little gain.

## Consequences

- `friend-body` and `art-direction` draw two views per part.
- The sketch's standing surgery should change (0021).
