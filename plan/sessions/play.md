# Play session: Mikey plays, notes go in

Mikey runs the latest build on his own laptop. No model is needed,
though a Sonnet chat at low effort can help turn rough notes into
tidy ones.

1. Pull `main`, build (`game/README.md`) and play what the row names.
2. Keep F3 on if the row is about speed. Write down FPS, frame ms and
   draw calls at a few spots, and which laptop it was.
3. Write a dated section in `plan/play-notes.md`: what felt good,
   what felt wrong, and any numbers changed in the Inspector (with
   their new values).
4. Update `STATE.md`: delete the row and flip the rows it unblocks to
   **ready**.
