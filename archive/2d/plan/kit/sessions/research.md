# Research session: learn from other games

**Tier:** F, ideally with web search. **With:** nobody, or Mikey.

## Purpose

Find out how other games and developers solved a problem we face, and
write down what's worth stealing and what to avoid.

## Pack with

The question in the task, the system doc it serves, and
`00-vision/references.md`.

## How to run it

1. Restate the question. Name the three to six games, talks or
   articles most likely to answer it, and say why each.
2. For each: what they did, how it played, and what it means for us.
   Separate **what you know** (with a source) from **what you
   remember** (mark it `[unverified]`).
3. End with: steal this, avoid this, and still unknown.

## Output

`research/YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>.md` (under 200 lines), with **Sources**.
New references go in `00-vision/references.md` as one line each.
Return the files, the log note and **Weak spots**.
