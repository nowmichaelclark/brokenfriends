## Item N: <short title>

For the build phase, once planning is done. Each item is one Claude
Code session's work.

**Why:** the player-facing reason, and the locked doc it comes from.

**What:** the change, concretely.

**Yardsticks:** how to tell it worked: tests, measurements, or what
Mikey will check on his phone.

**Must not change:** what has to stay exactly as it is.

**Out of scope:** the tempting extras.

**Stop and ask if:** the conditions where the session should leave a
note instead of guessing.

**Done:** filled in when merged: PR number, date, one line on what
happened. Finished items move to the archive.
