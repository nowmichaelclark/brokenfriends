# Play

## In charge of

Toys, minigames, and how friends grow their skills and stats (and may
learn new skills) by playing.

## Not in charge of

- Poking, feeding and the other care gestures: `care-touch`.
- Needs like energy that limit how much play is possible:
  `needs-growth`.
- Using the skills on quests: `expeditions`.

## Seed notes (from Mikey, 2026-10-24)

- "Play" instead of "train" (decision 0017): "Train sounds like a gym;
  yes their stats can increase from playing, but it is still largely
  playing through which the friends develop their skills or perhaps
  even learn new skills."
- Friends should be fun to poke and listen to, with toys, like
  Nintendogs (Q-023).
- References: Nintendogs; Chao Garden and Monster Rancher for stats
  that grow.

- The play corner (recording 1, 2026-10-24): a small corner of the
  tent where a friend sits, sleeps, and plays with a ball or a small
  skateboard; later more toys (Mikey: "we could have say 100 toys").
- Toys need more than one interaction (recording 2): a ball hits the
  whole body, a foot sometimes kicks it back, a trained friend still
  drops it; friends could throw and kick it to each other. A
  trampoline lets a friend trip at first and bounce high later.
- Skill numbers stay mostly hidden; casual players just play (0033).
- Friends speak in noises that sound like talk but aren't, R2-D2
  style, not robotic voices (recording 1).

## First questions

- Which skills and stats exist, and what does each change on quests?
- How long is one minigame?
- How do toys differ from minigames?
- Can play wear a friend out or damage it?
