# 0034: Quests are a main promise, shown on a map

## Decision

- **Quests are a main promise** of the game and appear in roughly the
  first ten minutes. A player may still just stay at camp and play.
- **Risk is shown on a map**, as in Death Stranding: the route, a
  chance of success, and danger signs (rivers, spikes). The player can
  draw a long safe route or a short risky one, and add side trips (a
  treasure chest) that bring their own risk.
- **New friends arrive** looking for help or a home, or are brought
  home from a quest. Now and then (1 in 5 to 10) one brings a toy or
  supplies.

## Context

Q-012 and Q-020. Mikey: "Quests are definitely a main promise of the
game... you can't really progress at all in the game without your
creatures going out and doing quests."

## Why

- Quests feed everything scarce (0027) and open new camps (0035).

## Alternatives rejected

- **Quests as a side activity:** Mikey made them central.

## Consequences

- Resolves Q-012 and Q-020. The success chance must be shown without
  text (0032), likely as a picture.
- `expeditions` owns the map, routes and side trips.
