# Mikey's answers (2026-10-24, to Claude)

Raw material for the next Brain session. Mikey's words, lightly trimmed.

## On Q-021: can a creature die while the player is away?

"Absence itself isn't a sole cause for death but things are always
changing in Broken Friends. A creature that is never fed gets skinny,
they won't die from malnourishment, but they will be faster to rot if
there is mold present in the camp for example. If a player leaves before
cleaning up the mold from a rotting leg, then it could spread through
the camp. How does the player leave the camp? That is what determines
how likely loss is. If it is left clean and tidy and all of the
creatures are all well taken care of, then loss is very unlikely."

## On Q-023: a pillar for fun?

"Yes, creatures should be fun to poke and listen to. I'm picturing

that with the creatures."
