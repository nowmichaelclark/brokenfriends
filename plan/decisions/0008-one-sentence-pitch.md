# 0008: The one-sentence pitch

## Decision

The one-sentence pitch for Broken Friends is: "Broken Friends is a
mobile pet game about stitched creatures that fall apart."

## Context

The first Brain session (DeepSeek) worked out the pitch with Mikey, one
line at a time.

## Why

- Short, sharp and memorable; the hook arrives immediately.
- "Stitched" and "fall apart" are the two things no other pet game
  promises.
- "Mobile" carries accepted decision 0003 without a second sentence.
- The repair turn is left to the next lines of the pitch on purpose. If
  a reader only ever reads one line, this is the one that should stick.

## Alternatives rejected

- **"…stitched creatures that fall apart, and putting them back
  together":** softer, but longer and less sharp.
- **"…stitched creatures you care for, play with, and sew back together
  when they fall apart":** more complete, more generic.

## Consequences

Sets the first line of `00-vision/pitch.md`. Nothing else.
