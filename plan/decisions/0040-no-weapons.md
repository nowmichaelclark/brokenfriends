# 0040: No weapons; friends avoid and deter

## Decision

No swords, guns or fighting skills. Friends get through danger by
avoiding and deterring it: agility, and gadgets such as a claw shot for
caves and mountains. Wild animals can hurt them (a wolf tears off a
limb).

## Context

Mikey: "I'm leaning towards no, to be honest with you... I just don't
want to add like swords and guns to the game. It's just like too
cheesy. It ruins the mood." He is not against danger.

## Why

- Weapons break the tone; danger still feeds the doctoring.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Combat with martial skills:** not the game's mood.

## Consequences

- A non-goal: weapons. `expeditions` owns encounters and gadgets.
