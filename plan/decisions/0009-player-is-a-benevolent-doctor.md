# 0009: The player is a benevolent doctor, not a god

## Decision

The player is a character of sorts, closer to a benevolent doctor than
to a god: they care for and befriend creatures, then send them on
quests. Creatures are friends the player plays with and cares for, not
things the player makes.

## Context

The first Brain session asked who the player is and how they relate to
the creatures. Later the player turns out to have goals they can reach
through the creatures; those goals aren't defined yet.

## Why

- Authorship ("this is mine, I made it") framed creatures as objects.
  Friendship and care are the intended feeling.
- The doctor role makes ripping off a rotting part read as treatment,
  not violence or crafting.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Author or builder** (the player assembles creatures from parts):
  makes creatures things, and pushes the game toward a crafting game.
- **God or creator** (the player makes and owns creatures): too much
  power over them, and it clashes with attachment.

## Consequences

- Shapes `00-vision/pitch.md` (the player's role) and the pillars.
- A "make your own creature" feature may come later; nothing in the
  plans should rule it out (Q-013).
- The player's goals are a question for the quest and expedition
  planning.
