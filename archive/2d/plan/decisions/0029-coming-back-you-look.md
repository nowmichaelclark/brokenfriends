# 0029: Coming back, the player finds out by looking

## Decision

There is no report of what happened while the player was away. The
camp shows it: mud tracked across the floor, bugs, mold that spread to
another friend's arm, a friend back from a quest still soaked and
sitting weakly in the play area.

## Context

Q-022: pillar 3 needs a visible history; pillar 4 refuses a "you were
away 6 days" message. Mikey: "You just have to look... just like when
you come home and it's like, okay, did your dog make a mess or did it
not make a mess? You'll kind of just know."

## Why

- The body and the camp are the interface (pillar 1); a report would
  be text and guilt.

## Alternatives rejected

- **A summary screen or log:** text, and it reads as blame.

## Consequences

- Resolves Q-022. Every effect of time away must leave a visible trace
  (`condition`, `time-save`, `art-direction`).
