# 0043: Many mini games, beside surgery and quests

## Decision

The game has many real mini games (with a goal, something to get better
at, and a way to fail), not only toys. Some raise skills, some give
prizes, some both, and a mini game can hurt a friend.

## Context

After playing the sketch, Mikey: "It is clear that we need some sort
of mini game (not a toy but an actual mini game) apart from surgery and
sending creatures out on quests... always the objective should be mini
games that are the most fun." On Claude's list (fishing, tower of
friends, run the route, keepy-uppy, trampoline, jam session, patchwork
puzzle, hide and seek): "Yes, I love all of these... Some should
increase stats, some should give prizes, some should do both. Yes, a
mini game could hurt a friend." (`inbox/2026-10-25-friend-looks-and-minigames.md`)

## Why

- Toys alone don't give enough to do between care and quests.
- Skills from mini games follow 0033 (repetition, quietly).

## Alternatives rejected

- **Toys only (the sketch's ball):** "not a toy but an actual mini
  game."

## Consequences

- `play` covers mini games; `expeditions` gets route mini games: "one
  route mini game could be cross the river, frogger style, another
  could be climb over the rocks or swing across the gaps."
- Two skateboarding games: "an obstacle and grinding course like olli
  Olli and one is a half pipe where friends can practice their tricks
  on and go for high scores."
- Prizes from mini games must not undo scarcity (0027).
- A hurt friend shows its harm back at camp (0029).
