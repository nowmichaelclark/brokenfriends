# Non-goals

The things this game deliberately won't do, especially tempting ones.
Each with one line on why. All of these are decided; the decision is
in brackets.

## Tone fence

These carry the "wholesome" half of the tone. Pillar 5 carries the
"gross" half, and gross is never the problem: playing with gross
things is wholesome (0030).

- **No blood or gore.** The gross comes from stuffing, rot, mold,
  fluid and insects, not from meat. (0030)
- **No pain the player can't treat.** A friend can hurt, but the hurt
  is always something the player can see and fix. (0011, 0030)
- **No revive.** Death is final; a dead friend's parts live on, the
  friend doesn't. (0011, 0026)
- **No story campaign.** No plot the game has to resolve. One ending
  scene, when the last camp is unlocked, and play goes on after.
  (0030, 0035)

## How it treats the player

- **No corporate hooks:** no login streaks, no energy timers, no
  nagging notifications. The game is for fun, not engagement
  numbers. (0024)
- **No real-money shop or microtransactions,** nothing gated, no ads.
  (0010, 0014, 0018)
- **No walls of text.** The game plays without reading. (0032)
- **No stat screens to grind.** Skills grow quietly through play.
  (0033)

## What it isn't

- **No weapons or fighting skills.** Friends avoid and deter danger.
  (0040)
- **No online play for now.** Single player, fully offline; sharing a
  friend by a code is wanted. (0039)
- **No 3D, no side views.** 2D, front and back only. (0002, 0031)
- **No iPhone for now.** (0015)
