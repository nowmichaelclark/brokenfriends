# 0025: Any part can come off; parts wear out

## Decision

- **Any part can come off**, healthy ones too. A healthy part holds: it
  takes several hard pulls (about three), more for a strong part.
- **Donation leaves the donor without the part.** A friend that gives a
  leg hops or crawls on the one it has left; nothing else is taken.
- **A part can die on its own**: rotted through, soaked, torn up, a
  cracked button. Past that point it can't be repaired.

## Context

Playing the sketch, Mikey found only the rotten leg came off: "even
healthy limbs a player should be able to pull off". Q-016 asked whether
a donor suffers: "the creature you took the part from is going to be at
a disadvantage usually in some way... it sort of hops on one leg."
Recording 2 (`inbox/2026-10-24-recording-2-part1.md` and `-part2.md`), confirmed point by point on 2026-10-25.

## Why

- Tearing is the fun the game is built on (pillar 5); refusing it
  because a part is healthy felt wrong.
- The cost of donation is visible on the donor's body (pillar 1).

## Alternatives rejected

- **Only rotting parts come off:** what the sketch did; Mikey found it
  strange.

## Consequences

- Resolves Q-016. The sketch should change (0021).
- `friend-body` needs pull strength per part and movement without a
  part (hopping, crawling).
