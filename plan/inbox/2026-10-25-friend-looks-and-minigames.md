# Friend looks and mini games (2026-10-25, to Claude)

Raw material for a later Brain session. Mikey's words, lightly
trimmed. Said after seeing a sheet of 54 generated friends (nine of
each body plan, from `game/tests/gallery.gd --by_plan`).

## On the generated friends

Claude's read of the sheet: every plan shares one body shape, monsters
look like purple cats, floppy ears stick out sideways, the ghost-like
hem looks like feet, every eye is the same button, there's no hair,
and colours are random within a plan.

Mikey: "Yes, and every head is just a circle/oval. Anyways, we don't
quite need to do redesigns yet, but yeah these designs would be nice
to change and it would be nice if friends could have fur and maybe
different fur lengths and fur in some places but not in others or even
fur all over."

## On mini games

"It is clear that we need some sort of mini game (not a toy but an
actual mini game) apart from surgery and sending creatures out on
quests. We would want to do some planning and brainstorming though
about what would be some good mini game ideas to add: always the
objective should be mini games that are the most fun."
