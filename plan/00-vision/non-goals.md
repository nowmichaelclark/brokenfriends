# Non-goals

To be written in Phase 1. The things this game deliberately won't do,
especially tempting ones. Each with one line on why.

## Tone fence (from decision 0012: Mikey to keep or cut)

These carry the "wholesome" half of the tone. Pillar 5 carries the
"gross" half.

- **No blood or gore.** The gross comes from stuffing, rot, mold and
  insects, not from meat.
- **No pain the player can't treat.** A creature can hurt, but the hurt
  is always something the player can see and fix (decision 0011).
- **No revive.** Death is final (decision 0011).
- **No story campaign.** No plot the game has to resolve, and no
  cutscenes carrying the meaning: caring for the creatures is the
  point.

## Other candidates to discuss (not decided)

- **Multiplayer.**
- **3D.** Already refused by decision 0002.
- **Real-money shops and microtransactions.** Already refused by
  decision 0010.
- **Combat.**
- **A management layer:** rotas, schedules, quotas. The camp chore is
  one tap, not a rota.
