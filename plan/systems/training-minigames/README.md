# Training and minigames

## In charge of

The minigames the player plays with a creature, and the stats or
skills each one trains.

## Not in charge of

- Needs like energy that limit how much training is possible:
  `needs-growth`.
- Using the trained skills: `expeditions`.

- Reference: Chao Garden and Monster Rancher training.
