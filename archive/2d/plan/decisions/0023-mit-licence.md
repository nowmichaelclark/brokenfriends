# 0023: The MIT licence

## Decision

The game is released under the MIT licence: code, and the art and
music its code makes.

## Context

Q-025 asked which open-source licence to use (0018: open source, free,
no ads). Mikey: "I don't know anything about open source licensing. All
I know is I want my game to be open and free and anyone can modify it",
and respected by programmers. Offered MIT or GPL, he chose MIT.

## Why

- Short, widely known and respected; anyone may use, change and share.
- Godot is MIT, and so is Driftloom's core, which the game embeds.

## Alternatives rejected

- **GPL:** keeps every changed copy open, but is stricter and puts
  some people off.

## Consequences

- A `LICENSE` file (MIT, Mikey as copyright holder) goes at the repo
  root.
- Anyone may make closed games from the code; that is accepted.
