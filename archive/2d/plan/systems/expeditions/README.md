# Expeditions

## In charge of

Sending friends out to pick up and deliver packages: the routes, the
terrain, carrying, what can go wrong, time away and the rewards.

## Not in charge of

- The stats that affect success: `play`.
- Damage and wetness picked up on the way: `condition` applies them.
- What rewards buy: `progression`.

## Seed notes (from Mikey's first ideas, unconfirmed)

- Pick up and deliver packages, like the carrier robots in Death
  Stranding.
- Claude's suggestions: Pikmin-style weight affecting speed; expeditions
  that run while the app is closed, like Fallout Shelter.

- Players may carry only one friend to another camp, with slots
  unlocked later (maybe up to three), so friends stay behind to keep
  camps clean; Mikey may scrap this (recording 2, 2026-10-24).

## First questions

- Does the player watch or control a delivery, or only send and wait?
- Can a friend be lost or badly hurt on an expedition?
- Do several friends carry together?
