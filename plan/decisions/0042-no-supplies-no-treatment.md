# 0042: Without the supplies, some things can't be treated

## Decision

There is no remedy that never runs out. Without what a treatment needs
(thread for sewing, mold spray for mold), it can't be done until the
supplies are found, on a quest or brought by a newcomer.

## Context

Gate A (`reviews/2026-10-25-gate-a.md`) found that scarcity (0027) could
leave rot the player can't treat, against pillar 5, and proposed a basic
remedy that never runs out (Q-031). Mikey: "No, sometimes you cannot
treat things if you don't have what is needed like thread or mold
spray."

## Why

- Scarcity only bites if running out matters (0027).
- The player is never helpless: they can see the shortage coming
  (pillar 3) and send friends to find more (0034).

## Alternatives rejected

- **A free basic remedy (Gate A's fix 1):** makes scarcity toothless.

## Consequences

- Pillar 5 now reads "can act on" rather than "can always fix": the
  player always has a way to act (care, a quest), not always a cure.
- Closes Q-031. A low shelf must be visible in good time (0029, B3).
