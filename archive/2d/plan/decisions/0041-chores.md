# 0041: Friends can be given chores

## Decision

A friend can be given a standing job, such as keeping supplies (mold
spray, bug spray) spread evenly between camps. A friend working long
and hard can get hurt doing it.

## Context

Asked about schedules and chore management, Mikey: "you could send a
creature to like, make sure all the cleaning supplies are like, evenly
spread through the different campsites... the creature could get in
trouble doing this."

## Why

- Many camps (0035) need looking after without constant attention.

## Alternatives rejected

- **No chores, everything by hand:** many camps become a burden
  (pillar 4).

## Consequences

- Narrows the "no management layer" candidate in `non-goals.md`:
  standing jobs are in; rotas and quotas are still out unless decided.
