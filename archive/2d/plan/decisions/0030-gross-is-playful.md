# 0030: Gross is playful; the tone fence stays

## Decision

There is no line where gross stops being wholesome: enjoying gross
things is wholesome, like a child playing with food. The tone fence
stays as it is: no blood or gore, no pain the player can't treat, no
revive, no story campaign.

## Context

Q-004 asked how far tearing and rot can go. Mikey: "I don't really
think there is a line... it's wholesome to kind of enjoy gross things.
That's the great part about being a kid." He confirmed the four
non-goals of the tone fence.

## Why

- Mud, mold, slime and squish are the toys; blood and meat aren't in
  the game at all, so there is nothing to hold back.

## Alternatives rejected

- **A gross budget per scene:** treats the gross as a risk, not a
  pleasure.

## Consequences

- Resolves Q-004. The tone fence in `00-vision/non-goals.md` is now
  decided, with one ending scene allowed (0035).
