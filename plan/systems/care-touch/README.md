# Care and touch

## In charge of

Everything the player does to a friend with a finger: poking,
dragging, tickling, feeding, washing, drying, tearing and sewing, and
how each gesture feels.

## Not in charge of

- What the friend's body does in response: `friend-body`.
- What the action changes in the material: `condition`.
- Menus and buttons: `ui-ux`.

## Seed notes (from Mikey's first ideas, unconfirmed)

- Pull limbs apart; sew them back together.
- Claude's suggestion: sewing could be a small tracing gesture or
  minigame.

## First questions

- Which gestures exist, and what does each do?
- Is tearing something the player chooses, an accident, or both?
- What does the friend do when you're rough with it?
