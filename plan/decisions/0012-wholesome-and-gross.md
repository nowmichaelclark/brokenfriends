# 0012: "Wholesome and gross" splits between pillars and non-goals

## Decision

The positive test for the tone lives in the pillars (pillar 5). The
fence lives in `00-vision/non-goals.md`.

## Context

Q-014 asked where the tone is tested: in the pillars or the non-goals.
A tone alone tests nothing, and a fence alone only says what the game
won't do, so neither document could carry it alone.

## Why

- Pillar 5 states the positive rule: every gross thing is something the
  player does, or can reach and fix.
- The non-goals list the refusals: blood and gore for shock, pain the
  player can't treat, revive, a story campaign.
- Each document does the job it is good at, and the gate has something
  concrete to check in both.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Pillars only:** tone pillars go vague and can reject nothing.
- **Non-goals only:** it never pushes toward the gross fun the game
  actually wants.

## Consequences

- `00-vision/pillars.md` has five pillars, the fifth being the tone
  test.
- `00-vision/non-goals.md` gains a tone fence for Mikey to keep or cut.
- A feature can now fail in two ways: it breaks a pillar, or it crosses
  a non-goal.
