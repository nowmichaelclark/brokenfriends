# Art direction

## In charge of

The look: style, palette, materials, how creatures are drawn and
animated, and how wetness, mold and tears show.

## Not in charge of

- The rules behind what is shown: `creature-body` and `condition`.

## Seed notes (from Mikey's first ideas, unconfirmed)

- Stuffed, stitched creatures; references: the films *9* and *The
  Nightmare Before Christmas*.
- Claude's suggestions: 2D cut-out parts on a skeleton; a fabric and
  stitch look like LittleBigPlanet or Kirby's Epic Yarn.

- Mikey (2026-10-24): at least some, maybe a lot, of the art
  procedurally generated, the way Driftloom generates its music and
  cover art (Q-006).

## First questions

- Which art is generated, and which is drawn? (Q-006)
- Cute, eerie, or both?
- How much of the look can come from shaders instead of drawings?
