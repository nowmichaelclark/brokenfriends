# Pillars

To be written in Phase 1.

Three to five short statements that every feature is tested against.
A good pillar lets you hold up a feature and answer yes or no. For
example (an illustration, not a proposal): "Every touch gets a visible,
physical reaction."

For each pillar:

- **The pillar**, in one line.
- **What it means in practice**, in two or three lines.
- **A feature it would reject**, to prove it's a real test.
