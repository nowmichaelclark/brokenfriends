# 0015: No iPhone for now

## Decision

iOS is out of scope for now. Targets are Android, the browser, and
later perhaps native Windows, Linux and Mac (decision 0003).

## Context

Q-003 asked how much iPhone matters, given iOS builds need a Mac
[unverified, V-004].

## Why

Mikey: "No, let's forget about iPhone for now entirely."

## Alternatives rejected

- **iOS at first playable:** needs a Mac and an Apple developer account.

## Consequences

- No Mac, Apple account or iOS testing needed.
- iPhone players can still use the browser version.
- Revisit with a new decision if that changes.
