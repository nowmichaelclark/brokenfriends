# 0013: As many creatures as the player wants, across camps

## Decision

How many creatures to keep is up to the player. Each camp holds a
limited number, and a player can have more than one camp.

## Context

Q-002 asked whether the player keeps one creature, a few, or a
collection.

## Why

Mikey: "Yeah, up to the player. A camp can only hold so many, but there
can be multiple camps."

## Alternatives rejected

- **One creature, Tamagotchi-style:** too narrow for donation,
  keepsakes and sending creatures on quests.
- **A fixed small roster:** a gate on the player's own choice.

## Consequences

- Camp capacity is a real rule; its number is a tuning knob.
- Multiple camps are in the game, at least eventually. When they arrive
  (first playable or later) is still part of Q-018.
- More camps means more to keep clean; how that fits pillar 4 ("nothing
  holds the player") needs watching.
